[SYSROOT] is the root catalog of the namespace, the node from which every other catalog ultimately descends. Its body is thin: a single rule stating that SYSROOT catalogs the namespace. It is reached from [PRJCTION], whose rule that the first identifier segment belongs to SYSROOT records back to this node, and the catalogs that hang beneath it — among them the filesystem projection [FROID_FS], the external specifications [EXTSPECS] and the realization options [EGO_OPTS] — each answer this one cataloguing rule.
