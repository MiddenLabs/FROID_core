# 00-00-00.01-01-00.00-00-00

[TIME] is the catalog of encoded times held by [PROJTIME], the place where each time described by [TIM_TIME] becomes a real node in this namespace root. Its one rule says that it catalogs encoded times, so its body governs membership only: a moment exists in the record by having a node beneath [TIME]. How a moment is turned into a node identifier, including the sign convention, the twelve-digit padding and the splitting of those digits across identifier blocks, is governed by [TIM_TIME] and not restated here. Each entry is a thin node whose substance is the set of folios deposited at that moment.
