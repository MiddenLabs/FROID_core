# 00-00-00.01-00-00.00-00-00

[PROJTIME] is the live instantiation, inside this namespace root, of the time projection defined abstractly as [PROJ_TIM]. Its one rule says that it carries the [TIME] catalog, so its body governs placement rather than content: the abstract projection defines how a time is encoded and rendered, and [PROJTIME] is where this root actually keeps the encoded times. It sits directly under [SYSROOT] beside the other instantiated projections, making time a standing dimension of the namespace rather than a field on individual records. The encoded times themselves are cataloged below it in [TIME].
