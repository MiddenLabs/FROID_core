[PROJOWN] is the live instantiation, inside this namespace root, of the ownership projection defined abstractly as [PROJ_OWN]. Its one rule says that it carries the [OWNER] catalog, so its body governs placement rather than content: the abstract projection says what an ownership catalog is, and [PROJOWN] is where this particular root actually keeps one. It sits directly under [SYSROOT] alongside the other instantiated projections, which is what makes ownership a standing dimension of the namespace rather than a field on individual records. Everything specific about who the owners are lives below it in [OWNER].
