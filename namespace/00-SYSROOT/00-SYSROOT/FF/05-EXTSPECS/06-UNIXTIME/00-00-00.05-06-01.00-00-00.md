# 00-00-00.05-06-00.00-00-00

[UNIXTIME] is a count of seconds since the epoch. Its body carries a single rule: UNIXTIME is defined by POSIX.1-2017, with the address of the relevant section recorded in the node's notes. It is a member of [EXTSPECS], the catalog of external specifications, and answers that node's cataloguing rule. [TIM_TIME], the node describing a time of a namespace root, cites it in several of its rules, so the times the record carries are counted as this node defines.
