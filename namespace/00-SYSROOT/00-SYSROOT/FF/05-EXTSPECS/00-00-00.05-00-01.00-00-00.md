# 00-00-00.05-00-00.00-00-00

[EXTSPECS] is a catalog of external specifications — the standards the system adopts from outside itself rather than defining in its own rules. Its body carries a single rule: EXTSPECS catalogs external specifications. It hangs directly from [SYSROOT] and answers that node's rule that SYSROOT catalogs the namespace. Each of its children names one adopted standard and pins it: [SHA256], [UTF8], [MKDNLINT], [X660], [UNIXRTRN] and [UNIXTIME].
