[SHA256] is the hash function used by the system, and its body is a short set of rules pinning both the function and the written form of its output. One rule fixes the definition to the external standard FIPS 180-4, whose address the node records in its notes; the other two require a digest to be written in lowercase hexadecimal and to be sixty-four characters long. The node is a member of [EXTSPECS], the catalog of external specifications, and answers that node's cataloguing rule. Its rules are leaned on widely across the record — the nodes that stamp and check body, manifest and index digests, among them [FRD_BODY], [FRD_MFST] and [FRD_INDX], all defer to it — so the digest form stated here is the form the whole namespace writes.
