[X660] is the object identifier standard used by the system. Its body carries a single rule: X660 is defined by ITU-T X.660. It is a member of [EXTSPECS], the catalog of external specifications, and answers that node's cataloguing rule. It is cited by [NODE_ID], whose rules require that an identifier convert to an X660 object identifier, and by [OIDCHECK], the checker overlay whose terms cover validating and constructing that identifier.
