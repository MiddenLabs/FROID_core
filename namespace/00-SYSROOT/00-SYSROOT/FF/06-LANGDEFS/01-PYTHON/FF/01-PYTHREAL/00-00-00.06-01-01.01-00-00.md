# 00-00-00.06-01-00.01-00-00

[PYTHREAL] is an overlay node: it has no body of its own describing an object, only a seam that states the terms on which a tool is bound to a language. Its two terms are narrow. The first says that a tool of [FRD_TOOL], the namespace root's tooling, when realized under this overlay is realized in [PYTHON]; the second says that this overlay is the default configuration of [PYTHON]. The second term is the reciprocal of the configuration catalog rule in [PYTHON] and takes effect through the first-position ordering rule held by [LANGDEFS]. Nothing else is exposed at this seam: the overlay selects a language for a realization and says nothing about how the tool is written.
