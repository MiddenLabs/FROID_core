[ERR_STD] is the option profile that directs every error to standard error, and it is written as a seam, so its body states a term of contract rather than a constraint. That single term is the whole of the contract: a tool of [FRD_TOOL] realized under ERR_STD sends every error it emits to standard error. The body names no second channel, no acceptance or refusal conditions and no exit grades of its own; how a tool exits is left to [FRD_TOOL], which requires its tools to exit per [UNIXRTRN] and to exit non-zero on a failed validation. ERR_STD is a member of [ERRORS], the catalog of error handling options, and answers that node's cataloguing rule.
