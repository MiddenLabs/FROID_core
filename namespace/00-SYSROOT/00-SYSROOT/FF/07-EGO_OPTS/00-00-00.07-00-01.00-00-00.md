[EGO_OPTS] is a catalog of language-agnostic realization option categories — the choices a realization must settle that do not depend on the language it is realized in. Its body carries a single rule: EGO_OPTS catalogs option categories. It hangs directly from [SYSROOT] and answers that node's rule that SYSROOT catalogs the namespace. Its own children are the categories themselves, among them [ERRORS], the catalog of error handling options.
