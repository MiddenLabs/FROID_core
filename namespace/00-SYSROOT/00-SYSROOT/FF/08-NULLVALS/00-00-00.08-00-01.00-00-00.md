[NULLVALS] is a catalog node sitting directly under [SYSROOT], and it exists to collect the values the system uses to express absence. Its single rule states that the node catalogs the values of absence, so its body governs membership rather than any behavior of its own: whatever the record uses to mean "nothing here" is expected to be registered beneath this node rather than invented locally. In the present record that catalog holds two entries, the literal value of absence and the digest of no input at all, each of which carries its own body of rules. Because [NULLVALS] is cataloged in turn by [SYSROOT], it is one of the top-level divisions of the namespace rather than a detail of any one projection.
