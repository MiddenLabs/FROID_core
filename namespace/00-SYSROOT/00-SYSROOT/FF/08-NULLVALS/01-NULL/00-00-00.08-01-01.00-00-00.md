[NULL] is the value of absence itself, the entry in the [NULLVALS] catalog that names how the record says a thing is not there. Its rule fixes the written form: where absence is meant, the value read is the word null, and no other spelling or empty placeholder stands in for it. Its restriction goes further and forbids leaving a field unpopulated at all, so a field with nothing to say must carry [NULL] rather than be blank or omitted. This makes [NULL] a required positive value rather than a gap, which is why it is referred back to from the manifest, body and index rules that decide what a field must contain.
