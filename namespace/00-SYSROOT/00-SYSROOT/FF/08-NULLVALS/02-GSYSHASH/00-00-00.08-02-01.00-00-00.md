# 00-00-00.08-02-00.00-00-00

[GSYSHASH] is the second entry in the [NULLVALS] catalog: the [SHA256] digest of no input, written out in full as a fixed constant so that the record has a stable way to say "there is nothing before this." Its rules state both that identity and that literal value, and its restrictions add that this node is never superseded and that it does not carry a back-reference for being named in a [MF_SPRUP] field, which is a deliberate exception to the general requirement that a referenced node record the reference. The point of that exception is practical: every folio at the head of a supersession chain names [GSYSHASH] as what it supersedes, so honoring the usual reciprocal rule would make this one node accumulate a back-reference from the entire namespace. [GSYSHASH] is therefore the anchor at the origin of every supersession chain rather than a participant in one.
