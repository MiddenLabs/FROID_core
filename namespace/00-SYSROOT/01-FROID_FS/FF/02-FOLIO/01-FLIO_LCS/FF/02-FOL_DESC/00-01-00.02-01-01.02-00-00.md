[FOL_DESC] is one of the three loci of control that [FLIO_LCS] allows for a [FLIO_ART]: the locus of an artifact authored outside the namespace. Its single rule states that origin, and its single restriction states the consequence — a descriptive artifact does not bind realization, which is precisely what separates it from a [FOL_NORM] artifact. The record still admits such artifacts into a folio and records the locus in the folio's manifest, so descriptive material can sit alongside binding material without being mistaken for it. A [FOL_REAL] artifact may be raised over a descriptive artifact just as it may over a normative one.
