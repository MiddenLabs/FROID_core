[FOL_NORM] is one of the three loci of control that [FLIO_LCS] allows for a [FLIO_ART]: the locus of an artifact that prescribes intent. Its body carries a single rule, which is that a normative artifact binds realization — what it states must be honored when the node it belongs to is realized. This is the property the other two loci lack, since a [FOL_DESC] artifact explicitly does not bind and a [FOL_REAL] artifact does not bind until the owner has reviewed it. Elsewhere the record narrows this binding for overlays, where a [FOL_OVLY] artifact binds only the realization it was selected for, and a realization decision may be promoted into this locus when it is deposited.
