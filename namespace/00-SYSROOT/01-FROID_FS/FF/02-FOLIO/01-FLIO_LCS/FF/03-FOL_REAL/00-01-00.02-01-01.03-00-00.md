# 00-01-00.02-01-00.03-00-00

[FOL_REAL] is the third of the loci of control that [FLIO_LCS] allows for a [FLIO_ART]: the locus of an artifact that records a realization decision no existing [FOLIO] specifies. Its rules make such an artifact a placeholder raised by the [OWN_OWNR] over a [FOL_NORM] or a [FOL_DESC], kept in the directory of the folio it is raised over, awaiting the owner's review and covering in the meantime whatever realization decisions depend on it. It is itself a [MODE_TRE] body, which is how the record admits a realized decision that neither the [MODE_ONE] nor any [MODE_TWO] of the node accounts for. At deposition it is disposed of in one of three ways — promoted to a normative artifact, deposited as a [FOL_BUG] where the decision is one observable at a seam, or discarded — and its restrictions state that it does not bind before review and does not survive deposition in its own right. The body also records a failure mode: an artifact of this kind that merely restates a decision some existing folio already specifies.
