[FLIO_LCS] is the node that names the locus of control of a [FLIO_ART] — where the authority over that artifact sits. Its body carries a single rule, which closes the set of possible loci to three: [FOL_NORM] for an artifact that prescribes intent, [FOL_DESC] for one authored outside the namespace, and [FOL_REAL] for one that records a realization decision no folio specifies. The node itself states nothing about what each locus does; those obligations live in the three child nodes, and a folio's manifest records which of the three applies to a given artifact.
