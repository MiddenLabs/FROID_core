# 00-01-00.02-03-00.03-00-00

[FOL_TYPE] is one of the four artifact roles that [FLIO_ART] enumerates: the content of a [FOLIO], held in one of the file types the record catalogs as [TYPE_TYP]. Its three rules delegate each kind of content to the node that defines it — a body to [FRD_BODY], an abstract to [FRD_SMRY], and any other folio artifact to [FRD_MISC]. The counts come from elsewhere: [FOLIO] requires one body in either mode and at most one for each mode, and exactly one abstract. This node therefore settles what kinds of content a folio may carry, not how many or how each is written.
