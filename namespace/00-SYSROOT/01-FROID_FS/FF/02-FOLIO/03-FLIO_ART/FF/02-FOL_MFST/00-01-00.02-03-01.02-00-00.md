# 00-01-00.02-03-00.02-00-00

[FOL_MFST] is one of the four artifact roles that [FLIO_ART] enumerates: the attestation and manifest of the artifacts of a folio. Its body carries a single rule, which delegates the whole of its definition to [FRD_MFST] — the fields the manifest must carry, their order, and when its hashes are written are all settled there rather than here. Its place in the record is fixed by [FOLIO], which requires one and only one manifest per folio, making this the artifact that vouches for the rest.
