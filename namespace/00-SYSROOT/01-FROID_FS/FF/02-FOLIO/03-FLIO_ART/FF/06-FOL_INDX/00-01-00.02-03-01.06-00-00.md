# 00-01-00.02-03-00.06-00-00

[FOL_INDX] is one of the four artifact roles that [FLIO_ART] enumerates: the artifact that indexes its own [FOLIO] and every folio beneath it. Its rules delegate the file's form to [FRD_INDX] and then govern its contents — each [NODE_ID] listed is unique, the index lists the tag of its own folio, and every tag below it is listed either directly or in the index of a node it lists, so the whole subtree is reachable. A namespace root folio must carry one, and an identifier may be added to an index without superseding the folio that holds it, so the index can grow as the namespace grows. Its restrictions make the listing permanent and stable: an identifier is never removed, a listed tag is never changed, and no entry is ever erased even when a folio is superseded; a tag reachable from neither the root index nor a chain of indexes leading from it is never valid. The failure modes named are a duplicated identifier, a recorded hash that does not match the folio it stands for, a removed identifier, and an orphan tag no listed chain reaches.
