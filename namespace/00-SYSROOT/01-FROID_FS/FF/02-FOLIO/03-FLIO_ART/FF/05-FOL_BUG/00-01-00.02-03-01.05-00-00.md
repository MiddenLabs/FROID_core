[FOL_BUG] is one of the four artifact roles that [FLIO_ART] enumerates: the artifact that acknowledges, in the record itself, that a folio's rules do not resolve cleanly. Its body carries a single rule, which delegates its definition to [FRD_BUG], where it is settled as a freeform, non-binding record that carries no rules or restrictions of its own. Its use is fixed elsewhere: a [FOL_REAL] placeholder may be deposited in this role, which is how an unresolved realization decision observable at a seam is preserved rather than silently settled.
