[FOLIO] is the node that defines a folio: the collection of [FLIO_ART] artifacts that together describe one [NODE]. Its rules fix what a folio must and may hold — exactly one [FOL_MFST], exactly one [FOL_TYPE] of [FRD_SMRY], one [FOL_TYPE] of [FRD_BODY] in either [MODE_ONE] or [MODE_TWO] with at most one for each of those two modes, at most one [FOL_INDX], and any number of further artifacts beyond those. Further rules govern a folio's life before it is fixed: while undeposited it may be revised and may be given a different [NODE_ID], and it enters the namespace only at deposition. Its restrictions make deposition final, so the content of a deposited folio is never altered, a deposited folio is never reassigned to a different identifier, and the namespace itself never changes a folio. Everything the record says about manifests, indexes, summaries and bodies attaches to a node through this container.
