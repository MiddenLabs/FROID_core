# 00-01-00.02-02-00.02-00-00

[FOL_OVLY] is the second of the two attachments that [FLIO_TCH] allows for a [FLIO_ART]: the attachment of an artifact that governs a node's realization from outside that node's own specification, without affecting structure. Its rules state that such an artifact applies to a [NODE] regardless of where the node sits in the namespace, that an overlay node carries a [MODE_TWO] and no [MODE_ONE], and that this seam is what does the governing. A fourth rule limits the reach of the overlay: it binds only the realization it has been selected for, which narrows the general obligation that a [FOL_NORM] artifact binds realization. Its restriction confirms the division of labor with [FOL_STRC] — an overlay defines no namespace position. The body's own example of such an overlay is a [MARKDOWN] profile that permits only unordered lists and ASCII characters.
