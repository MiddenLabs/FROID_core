# 00-01-00.02-02-00.00-00-00

[FLIO_TCH] is the node that names how a [FLIO_ART] attaches to the namespace. Its body carries a single rule, which closes the set of attachments to two: [FOL_STRC], for an artifact that is part of the namespace structure and fixes its node's position, and [FOL_OVLY], for an artifact that governs a node's realization from outside without touching structure. The distinguishing obligations belong to those two child nodes; this node only guarantees that every artifact is one or the other, and the folio's manifest records which.
