[MODE_TWO] is the mode that constrains which aspects of a [NODE] are exposed at its seam, that is, the contract surface rather than the internal account. Its rules allow a seam body to restate an aspect of the node's [MODE_ONE], require that it expose that first mode's contract surface, permit more than one seam to overlay a single first-mode body, and accept that some leakage across the seam is tolerated. A seam whose node has no first-mode body is instead the interface of an overlay node, and a seam body's definition statement must match the first-mode definition statement of the same node. In shape, a seam body carries contract terms in place of affirmative rules and takes at most one block of restrictions. The body also records a failure: a structural leaf node carrying a seam body is out of order.
