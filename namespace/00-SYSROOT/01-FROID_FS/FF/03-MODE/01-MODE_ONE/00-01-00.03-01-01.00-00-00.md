[MODE_ONE] is the mode that describes an object itself and its internal structure, as opposed to the surface it offers to others or the form it is eventually realized in. Its rules establish precedence: a body written in this mode outranks any other artifact of the same [NODE], and its definition statement outranks the definition statement of that node's [MODE_TWO]. A third rule fixes the shape of such a body, which carries affirmative rules in the block reserved for rules rather than the contract terms a seam would expose. The effect is that the first mode is the authoritative internal account of a node, and the seam and realization modes are read against it.
