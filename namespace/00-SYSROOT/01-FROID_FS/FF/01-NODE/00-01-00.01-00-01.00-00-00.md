[NODE] is the spec object that a [FOLIO] describes, the thing the record is about wherever a folio exists. Its own body is thin and purely organizational: its single rule states that it catalogs identifier grammar, so its substance is delegated to its children, chiefly [NODE_ID] for the identifier and [NODE_FNC] for the functions an identifier segment can take. It is one of the most widely cited nodes in the record, since folios, bodies, overlays, reciprocals, and the rule and term blocks all define themselves in relation to the node they govern.
