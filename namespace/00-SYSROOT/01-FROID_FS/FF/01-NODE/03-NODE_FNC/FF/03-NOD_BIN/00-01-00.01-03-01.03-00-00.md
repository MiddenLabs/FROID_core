[NOD_BIN] is the segment function that selects at most one member of a catalog, and it is the mechanism by which an identifier says which variant of a node an artifact is. Its rules assign fixed catalogs to the first two blocks: the bin of the first block classifies the [MODE] and the bin of the second classifies the artifact kind, with a value of zero meaning unused, and the classification code used is the constraint value of the mode or kind being named. The rules then distinguish a body header, which sets both of those bins to zero, from an artifact filename, which sets them to the artifact's own mode and kind; an artifact whose kind is neither a body nor a miscellaneous folio artifact sets the first bin to zero, while a miscellaneous artifact sets it to either [MODE_ONE] or [MODE_TRE]. Beyond the second block, the catalog a bin selects from is assigned by the projection rather than fixed here. Its single restriction forbids a filename or body header from altering the bin of the third block, which is left to the projection that owns it.
