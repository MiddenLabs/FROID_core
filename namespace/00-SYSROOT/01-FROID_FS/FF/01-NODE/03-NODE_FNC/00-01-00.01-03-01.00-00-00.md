[NODE_FNC] is the class of functions a [ND_SGMNT] can take, that is, what a given segment position in an identifier is doing. Its body is purely organizational: its single rule states that it catalogs segment functions, and the substance sits in its children, [NOD_CSTR] for segments that narrow meaning down the tree and [NOD_BIN] for segments that select a member of a catalog. Only its parent [NODE] cites it, so it serves as a heading under which the two functional kinds are defined rather than as a node that constrains anything itself.
