[NOD_TAG] is the human readable short name that stands in for a [NODE_ID], the form a person uses when citing a node in prose. Its rules require that a tag map to one and only one identifier, that it be chosen for the node rather than derived from the identifier, and that it be composed only of capital letters, digits, and underscores. Its single restriction caps a tag at eight characters. Because the bracketed names used throughout the record are these tags, the node is cited by the index and manifest nodes that build and hold the tag-to-identifier mapping and by the reciprocal block, whose back edges are recorded in tag form.
