# 00-01-00.01-02-00.01-00-00

[NOD_BLOK] is one block of a [NODE_ID], built from a fixed number of segments joined by hyphens, and it is also the catalog of the block's parts. Its rules set the block's shape at the moment a projection is instantiated: both the block width and the number of closing bin segments are decided then, the width must exceed the number of bins, and there must be at least one bin. Every block closes with its bin segments, and every segment before them is a constraint segment, so the division between [NOD_CSTR] and [NOD_BIN] within a block follows from the two widths alone. This node is heavily cited by the identifier and block conversion procedures and by the time projection, which all depend on knowing where a block's constraint part ends and its selector part begins.
