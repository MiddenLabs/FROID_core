# 00-01-00.01-02-00.01-01-00

[ND_SGMNT] is the smallest unit of a [NOD_BLOK], the individual value that identifier blocks are built from. Its rules require a segment to be an uppercase hexadecimal value of at least two digits, with the segment width fixed once for the whole namespace at instantiation. Its restrictions forbid a segment consisting entirely of F characters, which is reserved, and cap the segment width at thirty-two hexadecimal digits. The body enumerates the corresponding failures in full, covering a width below two digits or above thirty-two, a character outside the hexadecimal set, a lowercase character, and an all-F value, and it gives worked examples of each. It is cited by the segment, block, and identifier conversion procedures and by the projection nodes, all of which validate against these limits.
