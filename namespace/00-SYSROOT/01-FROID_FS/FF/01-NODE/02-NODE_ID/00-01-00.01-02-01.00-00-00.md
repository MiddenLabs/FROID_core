[NODE_ID] is the identifier of a node, constructed from the blocks its projection defines and joined by periods. Its rules govern what may hang off an identifier and how it is read elsewhere: an identifier may carry any number of short tags, requires at most one folio, and an identifier with no folio is a reference point that may stand as a placeholder for a folio written later. A further group of rules fixes its equivalence to an object identifier under the standard the system uses, with each hexadecimal value rendered as a decimal arc and both separators normalized to a single one. A final rule makes the node a catalog of its own parts, which is where [NOD_BLOK] and [NOD_TAG] are defined. The node is cited throughout the record by the manifest fields, the index builders, and the type and time nodes, because nearly everything else is addressed by its identifier.
