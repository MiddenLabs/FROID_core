[PROJ_OWN] is the part of a namespace root's projection catalog that holds the root's owners. Its two rules say only that it catalogs those owners and that a namespace root is instantiated with at least one of them, so a root never comes into being unowned. The owners themselves are described by [OWN_OWNR] beneath this node; [PROJ_OWN] fixes that they exist and where they sit in the catalog, not what an owner record contains.
