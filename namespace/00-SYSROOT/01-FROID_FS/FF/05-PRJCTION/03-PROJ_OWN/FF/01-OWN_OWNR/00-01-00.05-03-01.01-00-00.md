# 00-01-00.05-03-00.01-00-00

[OWN_OWNR] is the description of an owner of a namespace root. Its rules require that an owner be identified by a [NODE_ID] and that the record stand for a depositor of the namespace root, that is, the party in whose name material was deposited. Its restrictions draw the boundary tightly: the record does not authenticate a person or an agent, and it does not authorize a deposition; it names an owner and claims nothing further about them. Elsewhere in the record this is what a folio's owner field resolves to, and it is the party a realized artifact is raised by and awaits review from, and the party who picks a canonical second-mode artifact. The body notes that external identity providers would map an outside identity onto an owner record as a later overlay, not as core compliance.
