# 00-01-00.05-02-00.01-00-00

[TIM_TIME] is the description of a time belonging to a namespace root, and its rules define how such a time is written as a node identifier. The time is a [UNIXTIME], a count of seconds since the epoch as POSIX defines it, and it occupies the third, fourth, and fifth [NOD_BLOK] of the identifier: it is zero-padded to twelve decimal digits, cleaved into six two-digit pieces, and each piece fills a [NOD_CSTR] in big-endian order. The selector segment of the third block carries the sign, 01 for a non-negative time and 02 for a negative one, with a negative time encoded by its absolute value; the selector segment of every later block is zero. The restrictions hold the sign to those two values, cap the encoding at twelve decimal digits, and state plainly that the node does not attest the time it encodes, only records it. Each encoded time is cataloged by its projection, and the body notes that external attestation, such as timestamping authorities or ledger anchoring, would be a later overlay rather than part of core compliance.
