# 00-01-00.05-02-00.01-00-00

## 43fbb272bc969c665071e704f61af0f51344202867342cc397aeec7540291a71

## Definition

- [MODE_ONE]
- [TIM_TIME] is the description of a time of a namespace root

## Rules

- A01: [TIM_TIME] encodes a [UNIXTIME] as a [NODE_ID]
- A02: the [TIM_TIME] encoding is the third, fourth, and fifth [NOD_BLOK] of its [NODE_ID]
- A05: each <decseg> fills a [NOD_CSTR] in big-endian order
- A06: the [NOD_BIN] of the third [NOD_BLOK] is the sign
- A08: a sign of `01` is a non-negative [UNIXTIME]
- A09: a sign of `02` is a negative [UNIXTIME]
- A11: an encoded [TIM_TIME] is cataloged by its projection
- A12: the [NOD_BIN] of a subsequent [NOD_BLOK] is `00`
- A13: the [TIM_TIME] encoding zero-pads a [UNIXTIME] to twelve decimal digits
- A14: the [TIM_TIME] encoding of a negative [UNIXTIME] takes its absolute value
- A15: the twelve digits are cleaved into six 2-digit <decseg>
- A16: a <decseg> is a pair of decimal digits

## Restrictions

- B01: a sign is never other than `01` or `02`
- B03: [TIM_TIME] does not attest the time it encodes
- B04: the [TIM_TIME] encoding never exceeds twelve decimal digits

## Reciprocals

- MF_DEPST:A01
- MF_DEPST:A02
- PROJ_TIM:A01
- PROJ_TIM:A02
- PROJ_TIM:A03

## Examples

- E01: 1234567890 -> 001234567890
- E02: 001234567890 -> 00 12 34 56 78 90
- E03: positive 1234567890 -> 00-00-00.01-01-00.00-12-01.34-56-00.78-90-00
- E04: negative 1234567890 -> 00-00-00.01-01-00.00-12-02.34-56-00.78-90-00

## Notes

- external time attestation (RFC 3161 timestamping authorities, NTP-disciplined clocks, ledger anchoring) are future overlays that attest a [TIM_TIME]; they are not core compliance
