[PRJCTION] is the catalog of projections: the places where a namespace root records how it is rendered into a concrete form, and the definition of each such rendering. Its rules require that every projection be rooted at its own first node segment, that this first segment belong to [SYSROOT], and that the projection define the identifier blocks ([NOD_BLOK]) from which node identifiers in that projection are built. They also fix two naming facts about the folios at the top of a namespace: a namespace root [FOLIO] has a name ending in ROOT, and the folio for that root's filesystem projection has a name ending in _FS. Finally, the namespace root is instantiated once with each projection listed here, and what this catalog holds is the projection's definition rather than the projected material. The node is a first-mode description, so its body speaks in rules about itself; the projections it catalogs, such as the filesystem projection, carry their own bodies beneath it.
