# 00-01-00.04-02-00.08-00-00

[TYP_BLOB] is the entry in the [TYPE_TYP] catalog for binary or otherwise opaque content, and it works as the catalog's residual case: its rules make it an arbitrary sequence of bytes and give it every filename extension that no other entry in [TYPE_TYP] claims. Because such a file cannot carry a declaration inside itself, its rules say that its identity comes instead from its filename together with its listing in [MF_ARFCT], the manifest field that records each artifact of a [FOLIO] with the hash of its content. Its one restriction states plainly that no external standard governs it, which is what sets it apart from the other entries in the catalog, each of which adopts a named standard. This node is also the reason [TYPE] carries a restriction of its own, since the general requirement that a file type place its node identifier in the first available position has no first available position to apply to here.
