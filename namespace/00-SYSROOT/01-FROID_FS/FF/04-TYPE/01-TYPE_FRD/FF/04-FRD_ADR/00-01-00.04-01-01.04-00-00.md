# 00-01-00.04-01-00.04-00-00

[FRD_ADR] is a freeform Markdown decision record, one of the artifact types a folio may have. Its rules require that it be defined by [TYP_MKDN], that it record the decisions taken over its node, that it live outside the namespace while mirroring its node's path in [FROID_FS], and that each record within it be tagged with the letter R and a decimal number. Its restrictions keep it advisory: it does not bind realization, it contains no rules or restrictions, and it is not a folio artifact. Its reciprocals record that [TYPE_FRD] names it among the record types.
