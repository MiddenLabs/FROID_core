[FRD_BUG] is a freeform Markdown record, one of the artifact types a folio may have. Its two rules require that it be defined by [TYP_MKDN] and that its body be freeform. Its restrictions keep it advisory: it is non-normative, does not bind realization, and contains no rules or restrictions. Its reciprocals record that [TYPE_FRD] names it among the record types and that [FOL_BUG], the folio artifact that acknowledges a folio's rules do not resolve cleanly, cites it.
