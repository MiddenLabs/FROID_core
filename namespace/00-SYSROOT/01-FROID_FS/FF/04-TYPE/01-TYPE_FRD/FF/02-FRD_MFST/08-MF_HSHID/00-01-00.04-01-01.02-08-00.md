# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-08-00

[MF_HSHID] is the manifest field holding the [SHA256] over the folio's identity fields, the first link in the manifest's hash chain. Its rules declare the field to be a SHA256 value and fix exactly what is hashed and in what sequence: the text values of the node identifier, node tag, locus, attachment, owner, deposition time, and upward supersession fields, each terminated by a line feed. Its restriction is the complement of that list, stating plainly that this hash does not cover all fields of the [FRD_MFST], so the artifact and supersession hashes that may follow have their own scopes. It answers back to the manifest's field-order and terminal-hash rules and to the supersession hash that chains from it.
