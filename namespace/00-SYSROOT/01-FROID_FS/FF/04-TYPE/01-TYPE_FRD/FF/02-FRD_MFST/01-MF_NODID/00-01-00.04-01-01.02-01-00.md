[MF_NODID] is the manifest field that carries the [NODE_ID] of the [FOLIO] the manifest describes, written as a YAML field in the [TYP_YAML] content type. Its single rule requires the value to be a valid [NODE_ID], so the manifest names its folio in the record's own identifier grammar rather than by path or by prose. It is the first field of a [FRD_MFST] in the order that node fixes, and it answers back to that ordering rule. Being an identity field, its value is among those folded into the manifest's identity hash.
