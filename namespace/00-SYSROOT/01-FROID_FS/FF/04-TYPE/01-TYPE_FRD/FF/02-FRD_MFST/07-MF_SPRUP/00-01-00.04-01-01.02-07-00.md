# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-07-00

[MF_SPRUP] is the manifest field that anchors a folio's supersession chain upward, listing what the folio supersedes. Its rules require the value to be a list of valid [NODE_ID], make the listing reciprocal so that any folio named here records this folio in its own downward supersession field, and allow the entries to be either superseded folios or [GSYSHASH], the hash of no input, which stands for an origin with nothing behind it. Its restrictions forbid the field from being empty and forbid mixing [GSYSHASH] with a superseded folio in the same list, so a folio either begins a chain or continues one, never both. It is the seventh field of a [FRD_MFST] in the fixed order, and it answers back to that ordering rule.
