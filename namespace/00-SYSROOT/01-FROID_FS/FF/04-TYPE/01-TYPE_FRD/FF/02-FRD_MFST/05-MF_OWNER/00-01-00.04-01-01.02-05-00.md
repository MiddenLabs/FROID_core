[MF_OWNER] is the manifest field that names the [OWN_OWNR] who deposited the [FOLIO], written as a YAML field in the [TYP_YAML] content type. Its single rule requires the value to resolve to an owner cataloged by the namespace root of that folio, so the depositor is a recorded party within the same record rather than a free-text name. It is the fifth field of a [FRD_MFST] in the fixed order, and it answers back to that ordering rule. Because ownership is part of the folio's identity, its value is folded into the manifest's identity hash.
