# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-09-00

[MF_ARFCT] is the optional manifest field that lists each artifact of the [FOLIO] paired with the [SHA256] of its content. Its rules require that pairing, place every [FRD_BODY] artifact ahead of all others in the list, order those bodies by ascending node identifier taken from the filename, and require that the field be followed by [MF_HSHAR], the hash that chains over it. Its restrictions exclude three artifacts from the listing: the folio's index, its own manifest, and its decision records. A note observes that because first-mode artifacts sort before second-mode ones by ascending identifier, a folio holding only a second-mode body leads the list with it. A raised realization record over this node, not binding until the owner reviews it, proposes reading the phrase "content" two ways, taking a body's hash as the one its own body rules define and any other artifact's as the hash of its stored bytes.
