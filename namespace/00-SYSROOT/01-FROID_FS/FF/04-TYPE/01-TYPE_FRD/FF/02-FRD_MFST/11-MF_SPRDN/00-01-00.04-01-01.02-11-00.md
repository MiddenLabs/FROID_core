# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-11-00

[MF_SPRDN] is a field of a folio's manifest, written in the manifest's YAML type, whose value is the list of node identifiers naming the folios that supersede this one. Its body governs two affirmative rules: every entry must be a valid node identifier, and the field must be followed in the manifest by [MF_HSHSP], the hash that seals the supersession record. It is the downward-facing half of a pair with [MF_SPRUP], which anchors the chain from the other side, so a folio that lists a predecessor as superseded is in turn named here by that predecessor. The reciprocals record that [FRD_MFST] governs the field's presence and its placement among the manifest's fields, and that [MF_SPRUP] cites it.
