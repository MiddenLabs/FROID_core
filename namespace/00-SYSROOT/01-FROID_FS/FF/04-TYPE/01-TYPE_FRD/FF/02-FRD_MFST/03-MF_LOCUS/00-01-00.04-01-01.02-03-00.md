# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-03-00

[MF_LOCUS] is the manifest field that records the locus of control of the folio's artifacts, the [FLIO_LCS] that says whether they are normative, descriptive, or a raised realization record. Its rules require the value to be a valid [FLIO_LCS] and make the field an assertion about the folio's first-mode and second-mode artifacts, that is, about the body that describes the object itself and the body that exposes its seam. Its restriction withholds that assertion from third-mode artifacts, the realized outputs, which lie outside the namespace and are not covered by the folio's declared locus. It is the third field of a [FRD_MFST] in the fixed order, and it answers back to that ordering rule and to the field-validation seam that checks locus values.
