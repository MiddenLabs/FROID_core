# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-10-00

[MF_HSHAR] is the manifest field that extends the hash chain across the folio's artifacts, following [MF_ARFCT] whenever that field is present. Its rules declare the field to be a [SHA256] value and fix its input precisely: the prior hash in the chain, then, for each artifact in the order [MF_ARFCT] gives, that artifact's filename followed by the SHA256 of its content, with every value terminated by a line feed. Its restriction states that this hash does not cover all fields of the [FRD_MFST], leaving the identity fields to the hash before it and any supersession fields to the one after. It answers back to the manifest's optional-field and terminal-hash rules, to the artifact list that requires it, and to the supersession hash that chains from it.
