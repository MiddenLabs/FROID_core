# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-02-00

[MF_NODTG] is the manifest field that carries the [NOD_TAG] of the [FOLIO] the manifest describes, written as a YAML field in the [TYP_YAML] content type. Its single rule requires the value to be a valid [NOD_TAG], so the folio is named by its short mnemonic tag alongside the numeric identifier held in [MF_NODID]. It is the second field of a [FRD_MFST] in the fixed order, and it answers back both to that ordering rule and to the field-validation seam that checks tag values. Its value is one of those folded into the manifest's identity hash.
