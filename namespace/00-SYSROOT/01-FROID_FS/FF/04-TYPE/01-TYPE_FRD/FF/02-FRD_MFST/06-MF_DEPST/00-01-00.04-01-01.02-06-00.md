# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-06-00

[MF_DEPST] is the manifest field that records when the [FOLIO] was deposited, carrying a [TIM_TIME] value in the [TYP_YAML] content type. Its rules require that value to resolve to a time cataloged by the namespace root of the folio, so the deposition moment is expressed in the record's own time grammar, and they provide that a folio not yet deposited carries a placeholder time in this field instead of leaving it empty. It is the sixth field of a [FRD_MFST] in the fixed order, and it answers back to that ordering rule. Its value is among those folded into the manifest's identity hash.
