# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-12-00

[MF_HSHSP] is a field of a folio's manifest, written in the manifest's YAML type, carrying a SHA-256 digest chained from the prior hash together with the folio's superseding entries. Its rules require the value to be a SHA-256 digest and fix exactly what it covers and in what order, each value terminated by a line feed: first the digest taken from [MF_HSHID] and any following [MF_HSHAR], then the node identifier of each folio that supersedes this folio. Its single restriction states the field's deliberate narrowness, that it does not hash all the fields of [FRD_MFST]. It is the hash that [MF_SPRDN] requires after itself, and the reciprocals record both that dependence and the rules in [FRD_MFST] that govern the field's presence and ordering.
