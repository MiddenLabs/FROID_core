# 00-01-00.04-01-00.02-09-00

## 54024137add187b11c27a913c0e95d49b0f96c8ec34264d50d37c8066ebfc911

## Definition

- [MODE_ONE]
- [MF_ARFCT] is a [TYP_YAML] field listing each [FLIO_ART] of the [FOLIO] with its content [SHA256]

## Rules

- A01: [MF_ARFCT] is a list of [FLIO_ART] each followed by the [SHA256] of its content
- A02: every [FLIO_ART] of [FRD_BODY] precedes every other [FLIO_ART] in [MF_ARFCT]
- A03: [MF_ARFCT] orders its [FLIO_ART] of [FRD_BODY] by ascending filename [NODE_ID]
- A04: [MF_ARFCT] requires a following [MF_HSHAR]

## Restrictions

- B01: [MF_ARFCT] does not list a [FOL_INDX]
- B02: [MF_ARFCT] does not list a [FOL_MFST]
- B03: [MF_ARFCT] does not list a [FRD_ADR]

## Reciprocals

- FRD_MFST:A02
- MF_HSHAR:A02
- TYP_BLOB:A02

## Examples

- mf_arfct: [00-01-01.04-01-03.02-05-00.md,{SHA256},00-01-01.04-01-07.02-05-00.yaml,{SHA256}]
- mf_arfct: [00-01-01.04-01-03.03-00-00.md,{SHA256},00-01-02.04-01-03.03-00-00.md,{SHA256}]

## Notes

- [MODE_ONE] precedes [MODE_TWO] by ascending [NODE_ID], so a [FOLIO] with only [MODE_TWO] leads [MF_ARFCT] with it
