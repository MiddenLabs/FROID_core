# 00-01-00.04-01-00.06-00-00

## 1907772d387edf0f0c3ebc1ad705db0ebb71fcd9fef7b2ceadf40817d3c017b3

## Definition

- [MODE_ONE]
- [FRD_INDX] is a 1:1 mapping of [NOD_TAG] to [NODE_ID]

## Rules

- A01: [FRD_INDX] is defined by [TYP_CSV]
- A02: [FRD_INDX] is defined by `{NOD_TAG}.csv`
- A04: [FRD_INDX] declares its version number in a second row `mf_hshix,{SHA256}` :E01
- A05: the version number is the [SHA256] of the full [NOD_TAG] rows of [FOL_INDX], each line feed terminated
- A06: [FRD_INDX] contains [NOD_TAG] in column A :E02
- A07: [FRD_INDX] contains [NODE_ID] in column B :E02
- A08: [FRD_INDX] contains the terminal [SHA256] of each [FOLIO] in column C :E02
- A09: [FRD_INDX] is created with its [SHA256] fields reading [NULL]
- A10: [FRD_INDX] writes its [SHA256] fields at deposition
- A11: [FRD_INDX] orders [NOD_TAG] rows by [NODE_ID]

## Restrictions

- B01: no two [FRD_INDX] entries share a [NOD_TAG]
- B02: no [NOD_TAG] is `mf_nodid` or `mf_hshix`

## Reciprocals

- FOL_INDX:A01
- TYPE_FRD:A01

## Failure Modes

- D01: Duplicate [NOD_TAG] in [FRD_INDX]
- D02: `mf_hshix` does not match the [SHA256] of the [NOD_TAG] rows of [FOL_INDX]

## Examples

- E01:

  ```csv
  mf_nodid,00-01-00.04-01-00.06-00-00
  mf_hshix,{SHA256}
  {NOD_TAG},{NODE_ID},{SHA256}
  ```

- E02:
  - SYSROOT,00-00-00.00-00-00.00-00-00,{SHA256}
  - TYPE,00-01-00.04-00-00.00-00-00,{SHA256}
  - FRD_INDX,00-01-00.04-01-00.06-00-00,{SHA256}
