[FRD_SMRY] is the artifact kind that carries a folio's abstract: a prose statement, written in the markdown content type [TYP_MKDN], of what the node it belongs to is. Its rules require that it be defined by [TYP_MKDN], that it be realized from the artifacts of its own [FOLIO] rather than composed independently, and that it state the identity of its node in prose. Its restrictions keep it out of the normative path: an abstract does not bind realization, it carries no rules, terms, or restrictions of its own, and it is never a [FRD_BODY]. It therefore stands beside the folio's governing body as a readable description of it, and it answers back to [FOLIO] and to the artifact role that a folio's summary occupies.
