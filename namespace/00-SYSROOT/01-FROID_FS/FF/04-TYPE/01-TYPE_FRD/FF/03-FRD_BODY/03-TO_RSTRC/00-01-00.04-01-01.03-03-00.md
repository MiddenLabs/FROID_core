[TO_RSTRC] is the section of a folio body holding the constraints placed on the node, opening with the restrictions heading. Its rules fix the form of every entry, a tag made of the letter B and a two-digit decimal number, each constraint starting a new line behind a dash and its tag, each constraint a single negative statement about the node. Unlike a rule, a constraint may state a condition; a conditional constraint cites the rule it conditions by that node's tag and the rule's tag, and the conditioned rule yields within the constraint's scope. Its own restrictions hold each constraint to no more than one assertion, bar a constraint from containing rules, and release a restriction from the requirement to carry a matching entry in [TO_RCPRC]. The failure modes name a missing heading, a constraint of more than one statement, and a constraint that contains rules. The reciprocals record that [FRD_BODY] places this section in order and counts it toward nodehood, that a [MODE_TWO] body takes at most one of it, and that the one-statement and three-section checks cite it.
