# 00-01-00.04-01-00.03-04-00

[TO_RCPRC] is the section of a folio body holding the node's reciprocals, the back-references recording who cites it, and it opens with the reciprocals heading. Its rules fix the entry form, each reciprocal on its own line behind a dash, written as the tag of another node, a colon, and then the tag of the referencing rule, term or manifest field; a referencing node that has no tag of its own is cited by its node identifier instead. They also state the discipline this section enforces across the record: a node is referenced by tag or identifier in a rule, a term or a node-valued manifest field, and a referenced node carries a reciprocal of that reference, which may be an entry here or a reciprocal manifest field. A second kind of entry is membership, where the cited node's rule quantifies over a class containing this node, and such a member may carry a reciprocal of that rule. Its single restriction forbids a reciprocal from restating the rule it points at, and its failure modes name a missing heading and a reciprocal citing something that neither references this node nor quantifies over a class containing it. The reciprocals record that [FRD_BODY] places this section in order and counts it toward nodehood, and that the reciprocal-closure and three-section checks cite it.
