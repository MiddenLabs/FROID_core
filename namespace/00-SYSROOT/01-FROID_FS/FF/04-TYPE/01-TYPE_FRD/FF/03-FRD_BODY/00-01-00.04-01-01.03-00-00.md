[FRD_BODY] is the part of a folio that carries the list of constraints applied to a node, written in the record's Markdown type. Its first body governs the body's own shape: it is built from one or more named sections, the first of which is [TO_DFNTN], a parameter that another node sets is written in angle brackets, the sections appear in a fixed order and each appears at most once, tags are unique within the node, and a body carrying at least one rules, terms, restrictions or reciprocals section is thereby a node. That body also governs the identity stamp, requiring the body to declare its SHA-256 digest as a level-two Markdown heading on its third line, covering the text as stored from the first section heading to the end, with an unwritten stamp reading null; a body with no sections at all is named as a failure. The second body is the contract [FRD_BODY] exposes at its seam to whatever reads or writes a body: it accepts sections that are defined by the child folios of [FRD_BODY], that open as level-two Markdown headings, and that run in the order definition, rules, terms, restrictions, reciprocals, failure modes, examples and notes, with any empty section omitted rather than left standing. It refuses the two corresponding faults, sections placed out of that order and a section present with no content. Its reciprocals are the widest in this folio, recording that the folio and type nodes, the mode nodes, the manifest's artifact fields and the body's own validation nodes all reference it.
