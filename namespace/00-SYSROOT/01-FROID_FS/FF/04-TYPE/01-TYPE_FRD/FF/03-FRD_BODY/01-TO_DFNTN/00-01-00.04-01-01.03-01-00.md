[TO_DFNTN] is the opening section of a folio body, a Markdown block carrying the mode of the [FRD_BODY] and the single-sentence identity statement of the node. Its rules require the block to begin with the definition heading and to make exactly two statements, the first declaring the mode and the second declaring what the node is. Its restrictions keep the block bare: it holds no rules, restrictions or reciprocals, and it does not describe what the node does, only what it is. Its failure modes name the ways this goes wrong, namely a missing heading, a statement count other than two, a first statement that is not one of the three mode names, and a second statement that is not the identity. The reciprocals record that [FRD_BODY] requires this section first and fixes its place in the section order, that [MODE_ONE] and [MODE_TWO] govern how a node's two definitions relate and which takes precedence, and that the definition-hygiene checks cite it.
