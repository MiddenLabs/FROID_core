# 00-01-00.04-01-00.03-08-00

[TO_TERMS] is the section of a folio body holding the contract terms that a [MODE_TWO] body exposes at the node's seam, opening with the terms heading. Its rules fix the entry form, a tag made of the letter T and a two-digit decimal number, each term starting a new line behind a dash and its tag, and each term a single statement, affirmative or negative, about what the node exposes rather than about its internals. The section appears only in a [MODE_TWO] body, where it stands in place of [TO_RULES], and a term may write an operation of its own node in angle brackets. Its single restriction holds each term to no more than one assertion, and its failure modes name a missing heading, a term of more than one statement, and a term referencing another node without a matching entry in [TO_RCPRC]. The reciprocals record that [FRD_BODY] places this section in order and counts it toward nodehood, and that [MODE_ONE] and [MODE_TWO] together decide which of terms and rules a given body carries.
