# 00-01-00.04-01-00.03-02-00

[TO_RULES] is the section of a folio body holding the affirmative rules that apply to the node, opening with the rules heading. Its rules fix the form of every entry, a tag made of the letter A and a two-digit decimal number, each rule starting a new line behind a dash and its tag, each rule a single affirmative statement about the node. They also govern conditioning: a rule may quantify over a class and may condition another node's rule or constraint, citing that node's tag and the tag of what it conditions, and the conditioned rule or constraint yields within the conditioning rule's scope, which is its quantified subject. Its restrictions hold each rule to no more than one assertion, bar a rule from containing restrictions, and bar any condition beyond a conditioning citation, which is why a rule gated on words such as when, if, unless, provided or whenever is listed as a failure. A further failure mode is a rule that references another node without a matching entry in [TO_RCPRC]. The reciprocals record that [FRD_BODY] places this section in order and counts it toward nodehood, that a [MODE_ONE] body uses it where a [MODE_TWO] body would use [TO_TERMS], and that the one-statement and three-section checks cite it.
